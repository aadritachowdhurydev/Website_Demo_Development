A LITTLE ABOUT US

Different interests.

MOSAIC is a student-focused event hub concept for the Institute of Engineering & Management, Kolkata. It brings technical events, workshops, talks and cultural activities together in one accessible place.

Our aim is to help students discover opportunities, explore interests and participate in campus life.
